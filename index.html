About

Liam James Taylor — Norwich based writer. Finishing my MA in Prose Fiction at the University of East Anglia. My background is working as an assistant director in the UK Film and TV Industry, but I made the pivot to pursue a career in something more creatively rewarding a few years ago.

I'm interested in stories which attempt to translate human consciousness and experience through the written medium. My work uses experimental form to explore themes of grief, family dynamics, abandonement and community.

This site is a work in progress.
